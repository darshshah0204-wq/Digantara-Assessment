Projects

1) Orbit & Reentry Propagator

Problem: Needed an end-to-end simulation framework to model orbital propagation and atmospheric reentry behavior.

Approach: Implemented a two-body orbital mechanics solver and coupled it with drag-based reentry dynamics using ballistic coefficient terms.

Tools: MATLAB/Python for propagation and analysis, STK for trajectory visualization.

Results: Produced complete orbit-to-reentry profiles with interpretable altitude, velocity, and decay trends.

Validation: Compared simulated descent behavior against real-world reentry references to check model realism.

2) CFD Bluff Body Simulation

Problem: Quantify aerodynamic drag behavior around a bluff body in subsonic flow.

Approach: Built CAD geometry, generated refined near-wall and wake mesh regions, and solved steady RANS cases under realistic boundary conditions.

Tools: ANSYS Fluent (meshing + solver), post-processing for force and wake-field interpretation.

Results: Estimated drag coefficient and flow separation structures; identified wake-driven drag trends.

Validation: Benchmarked drag coefficient range against accepted literature/experimental values for comparable geometries.

3) SAE Aircraft Design

Problem: Design a competitive aircraft balancing aerodynamic efficiency, payload needs, and stability limits.

Approach: Evaluated airfoil and planform choices, iterated geometry for lift/drag trade-offs, and integrated mass property constraints.

Tools: SolidWorks, XFLR5, CAD-based design reviews.

Results: Achieved a mission-oriented configuration with improved aerodynamic consistency and structural feasibility.

Validation: Cross-checked aerodynamic outputs with stability margins and weight & balance targets.

4) Rocketry Project

Problem: Build practical system-level understanding of launch vehicle subsystems and experimental control logic.

Approach: Studied propulsion/avionics/recovery interfaces and developed Arduino-based experiments for sensing and subsystem behavior.

Tools: OpenRocket, Arduino ecosystem, subsystem test setups.

Results: Improved subsystem integration awareness and experimental readiness for rocketry workflows.

Validation: Verified behavior through iterative test runs and parameter checks during prototype experiments.
